Partner With YLDP

YLDP runs inside schools across Metro Detroit. Partner schools open their doors after the bell, and BMEA brings trained high school Fellows, adult site coordinators, and a mentoring program built around reading, math, and leadership. Here's what a partnership looks like and how to get started.

What We Ask of Partner Schools

A Home Base

A consistent room or gym for after-school sessions on program days, so mentees always know where to find us.

A Point of Contact

A staff member who works with our site coordinator on schedules, dismissal, and communication with families.

Student Enrollment

Help identifying the K-7 students who would benefit most from mentoring, and getting their families signed up.

Shared Data

Access to fall and spring reading and math assessments, so we can measure every mentee's growth.

A Typical Afternoon

Every site runs a little differently, but most afternoons follow the same rhythm.

  1. 3:30 PM

    Arrival and Check-In

    Fellows set up the space and greet mentees by name as they come in.

  2. 3:45 PM

    Snack and Circle

    A quick check-in on how everyone's day went before the work starts.

  3. 4:00 PM

    Academic Block

    Small-group reading and math support built around productive struggle and productive talk.

  4. 4:45 PM

    Enrichment

    Hands-on projects like engineering builds, plus lessons on Black leaders making a difference in the world.

  5. 5:15 PM

    Outdoor Play

    Basketball, football, and time outside. Mentees name play as one of their favorite parts of the program.

  6. 5:45 PM

    Reflection and Dismissal

    Mentees share one thing they're proud of, then Fellows walk them to dismissal.
