Partner With YLDP

YLDP runs inside schools across Metro Detroit. Partner schools open their doors after the bell, and BMEA brings trained high school Fellows, adult site coordinators, and a mentoring program built around reading, math, and leadership. Here's what a partnership looks like and how to get started.

What We Ask of Partner Schools

A Home Base

Sessions run best in a consistent space, so Fellows can set up quickly and mentees know where to go.

Safe and Structured Dismissal

Following the school’s release and pick-up process keeps students safe and matches what families already expect.

Homework Sent With Students

When mentees bring homework, Fellows help with real assignments students need in class.

Family Updates

Including BMEA in school messages gets program news to parents through a channel they already read.

A Typical Afternoon

Every site runs a little differently and at different times, but most afternoons follow the same rhythm.

  1. 3:00pm

    Prep Time

    Site Coordinators arrive at least 30 minutes before the session to set up and check in with a school staff member.

  2. 3:30pm

    Mentee and Mentor Arrival

    Elementary Mentees attend for 1.5 hours. High School Fellows stay for 2 hours, which adds a pre-huddle or debrief.

  3. 4:00pm

    Academic Block

    One High School Fellow mentors 1 to 3 Elementary Mentees. Each session follows the YLDP Afterschool Learning & Leadership Playbook (28 weekly projects).
