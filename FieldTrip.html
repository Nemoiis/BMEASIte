Field Trips

Throughout the year, YLDP mentees and Fellows travel together to museums, universities, and cultural sites across the region. This is where you'll find everything you need for each trip, including dates, locations, and details. A permission slip is required for every trip. Please review and sign the form below before the trip date.

March 24-27 2026

Washington, D.C. trip

Students will travel to Washington, D.C. to visit national landmarks, including the Martin Luther King Jr. Memorial, and explore museums that bring history to life outside the classroom. This trip is part of YLDP's commitment to giving mentees and Fellows real-world experiences that build confidence, curiosity, and a stronger sense of identity.
