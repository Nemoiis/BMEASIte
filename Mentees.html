Our Mentees

YLDP mentees are Black boys in grades K-7 growing as readers, thinkers, and young leaders. Every afternoon they work alongside high school Fellows who look like them, come from their communities, and remember what it was like to be their age.

In Their Words

What Families Noticed

In BMEA's Parent Perception Survey, 9 in 10 families said they saw positive change in their child. These are the changes they named most.

  • Improved confidence
  • Stronger communication
  • Increased leadership
  • Better behavior and decisions
  • Better peer relationships
  • More interest in school

"It was like his light was restored. His confidence returned, he began to excel in school... the program was exactly what my son needed."

BMEA Parent
