Data Collection

Data is how we show what families already see. Fellows and site coordinators use this page to submit attendance, assessments, and reflections throughout the year. Everything submitted here feeds into the annual impact report.

What We Collect

Attendance

Daily sign-in for Fellows and mentees at every site. Last year, Fellow attendance averaged 82.4%.

Assessments

Matched reading and math assessments in the fall and again in the spring, so we can track each mentee's growth.

Reflections and Surveys

Year-end reflections from Fellows, plus the Parent Perception Survey from families.

Where It Goes

From January to May 2026, the University of Michigan Marsal Family School of Education studied YLDP through a Critical Participatory Action Research partnership. Its finding: BMEA is a highly impactful, relationship-centered program, with identity, belonging, and mentorship as its core drivers of success. The data you submit is how we keep proving it.
