Our intergenerational mentoring model seeks to increases the math proficiency of Black Boys in grades K-5 by solving issues in their community and by creating a sustainable pipeline of Black males becoming civically engaged teachers.

Expand the Reach of BMEA's Youth Leadership Development Program by joining us in our mission to create liberatory school spaces for Black, Brown, and economically disadvantaged communities across the nation.

Together, we can broaden the impact of BMEA's Youth Leadership Development Program and empower the next generation to thrive personally and academically.


Our Focus


The Youth Leadership Development Program focuses on intergenerational mentoring to foster confident and skilled individuals while creating a pipeline for Black males in the teaching profession. The program utilizes high school mentors to engage elementary-aged students, aiming to improve math proficiency and develop emerging young Black male leaders by fostering productive struggle, productive talk, and cultivating mindsets. It's important to remember:


  • The Youth Leadership Development Program nurtures Black male identity and fosters a community of learners supporting each other’s growth

  • High school mentors play a crucial role in engaging elementary-aged students, creating positive Black male role models in teaching.

  • The program operates both during and after school hours, proactively supporting young leaders in their learning and fostering community building and skill cultivation.
