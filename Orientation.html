How YLDP Works

YLDP is an ecosystem. Every layer pours into the next, and the youngest give back just as much as they receive.

  1. 01

    Leadership and Staff

    Vision, structure, and care that hold the whole program.

  2. 02

    Adult Mentors and Site Coordinators

    Educators who model teaching and pour into Fellows.

  3. 03

    High School Fellows

    Paid young men learning to lead and to teach.

  4. 04

    Elementary Mentees

    Black boys in grades K-7 growing as readers, thinkers, and leaders.

  5. 05

    Families and Community

    Trusted partners who hold every layer of the program together.

Everything we do is grounded in four core values:

  • Love & Care
  • Integrity
  • Commitment
  • Execution

What You'll Grow In

The YLDP Competency Model is the set of skills every Fellow builds over the year.

Healthy Relationship Building

Learning about yourself and how to work with others.

Civic Engagement

Approaching challenges thoughtfully, with positive outcomes in mind.

Emotional Intelligence and Resilience

Confidence, managing stress, empathy, and perseverance.

Leadership, Advocacy, and Professionalism

Modeling leadership and teaching within BMEA's core values.
