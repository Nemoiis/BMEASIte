Our History

BMEA's Youth Leadership Development Program pairs Black and Brown high school Fellows with elementary-aged mentees across Metro Detroit, creating an intergenerational model where mentoring builds leadership, confidence, and a pipeline into the teaching profession. What started as a mentoring initiative has grown into a program serving multiple school sites, backed by an independent research partnership with the University of Michigan confirming its impact.
