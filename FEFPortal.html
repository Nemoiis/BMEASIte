Role and Expectations

Mentors show up consistently, model professionalism, and build real relationships with Fellows and mentees. We don't lead with punishment. We lead with care, high expectations, and accountability.

Benefits of Mentoring

Join an intergenerational community of educators, pour into the next generation of Black male teachers, and grow alongside Fellows who are learning to lead.
